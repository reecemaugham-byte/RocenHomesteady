🚜 Farm Tycoon

Build your farm from the ground up. Grow crops, raise animals, buy the Manor to win!

📖 Guide & Progression
  1. 📅 Seasons: Each season lasts 30 days (120 days = 1 Year). Animals unlock in Year 2.
  2. 🌱 Farming: Crops take 3 days to grow (Seed → Growing → Ready). Empty plots regenerate +5 soil health/day.
  3. 📦 Feed: Animals eat Feed Bags. Recipe: 1 Wheat + 1 Carrot + 1 Corn = 5 Feed.
  4. 🦅 Scarecrow: +1 yield to adjacent ready crops.
  5. 📉 Market: Selling >10 of one item crashes the price by 20%. Random surges double prices!
  6. ❄️ Winter: Crops die (unless Cold Frame built). Fish still available.

📖 How to Play
  1. 🖱️ Plant: Select a crop and click an empty bed.
  2. 💧 Water: Water crops each day (50p/bed) or wait for rain (free!).
  3. 🤝 Companion Planting: Good companions give yield bonuses. Bad companions reduce yield.
  4. 🔄 Crop Rotation: Same family in same bed = -30% yield. Legumes ADD nitrogen!
  5. 🌟 Golden Crops: 1 in 100 chance when harvesting! Worth 10x the price.
  6. ❄️ Winter: Crops die (except Herbs). Plan ahead!
