🌿 Foraging Quest

A narrative survival game. Identify real plants. Make life-or-death choices. How long can you survive?

📖 How to Play
  1. Choose a scenario — each has different challenges, resources, and plant life.
  2. Survive day by day — manage your Health, Hunger, Warmth, and Morale.
  3. Choose actions wisely — you have limited daylight hours each day.
  4. Forage carefully — identifying plants uses your real foraging knowledge. Correct ID feeds you. Wrong ID can be fatal.
  5. Respond to events — wildlife, weather, and discoveries. Every choice matters.
  6. Find your way out — signal for rescue, explore to find civilisation, or thrive in the wild.

⚠️ Real Knowledge Required — the plant identification in this game uses real foraging data. Correctly identifying plants keeps you alive. Mistakes have real consequences — just like in the wild.

⚠️ Safety Reminder
