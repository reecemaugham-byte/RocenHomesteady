🏘️ Eco-Village Builder

📖 How to Play
  1. Forage: Gather resources from the woods or sell items from Foraging Games. Rainy weather boosts forage yields!
  2. Build: Place buildings on your land to produce resources.
  3. Produce: Use Production to turn raw goods into valuable items.
  4. Market: Sell items for money. Processed goods are worth much more!
  5. 🌤️ Weather: Each day has weather that affects production. Storms disable solar/wind, drought reduces water, rain boosts forage yields.
  6. ⚠️ Winter: Solar Panels and Wind Turbines stop. Nature stops growing. Stockpile food AND wood!
  7. 🏠 Heating: Each House needs 1 Wood/day in Winter (2 in Cold Snaps!). Unheated homes take damage every 2 days!
  8. 🫧 Cold Frame: Produces +3 Food/day even in winter (reduced in drought).
  9. 🥓 Smokehouse: Produces +1 Wood/day (costs Nature). Cook Smoked Fish and Jerky without Power.
  10. 🐐 Goat Pen: Produces 1 Milk/day in any season.
  11. 🌱 Greenhouse: Produces 1-2 random crops (Strawberry, Tomato, Pepper, Cucumber, Lettuce) year-round.
  12. 📦 Storage: Personal inventory holds foraged items. Build a Barn to unlock Barn Storage for winter reserves.
  13. 🏗️ Barn: Use → to move items into the Barn. Village operations pull from Barn first. ← takes items back out.

💡 Tip: Dandelion Tea is worth 25× more than raw Dandelions. Invest in production!

🌧️ Weather Tip: Forage on Rainy days for the best yields. Avoid foraging during Storms.

📖 How to Play
  1. Book: Click the cookbook on the shelf to browse recipes and answer questions.
  2. Cauldron: After selecting a recipe, click the cauldron to cook it.
  3. Inventory: Your pantry is filled by Foraging Games. Go foraging first!
  4. Progression: Unlock 3 Beginner recipes to open the Intermediate chapter.
  5. Sell: Cooked dishes can be sold in Eco-Village for good money!
