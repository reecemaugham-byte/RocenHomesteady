☠️ Survival School

Identify plants, spot dangers, and build your foraging knowledge!

📖 How to Play
  1. Choose a difficulty: Patrol (no timer), Field Test (30s timer), or Survival (15s timer, 1 life).
  2. Each round presents a danger identification (safe vs dangerous) or a knowledge question (habitat, season, parts, etc.).
  3. Danger rounds: Choose the SAFE plant. Wrong = lose a life + see what would happen.
  4. Knowledge rounds: Answer correctly for XP. Wrong = see why it matters.
  5. Every 5 correct in a row triggers a Bonus Round for double points.
  6. Plants you get wrong go into your review queue — they'll come back until you learn them.
  7. Fill your herbarium: discovered (1×) → learned (3×) → mastered (5×).
  8. Reach Level 2 by solving 5 cases — harder plants and fungi appear!
