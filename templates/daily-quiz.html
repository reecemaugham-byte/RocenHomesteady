🎲 Daily Quiz

Test your plant knowledge across 5 categories!

📖 How to Play
  1. Choose a category or play All for a mix.
  2. Select difficulty: Beginner (3 options, 20s), Intermediate (3 options, 15s), or Expert (4 options, 12s).
  3. Correct answers earn 10 base points, plus streak and time bonuses. Higher difficulty = higher multiplier!
  4. You have 3 lives (or 1 in Challenge Mode). Timer runs out = lost life.
  5. Every plant you encounter is added to your Herbarium — master them all!
  6. Try the 📅 Daily Challenge — same questions for everyone, compare scores!
